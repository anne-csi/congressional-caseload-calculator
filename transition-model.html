How this works. Cases open evenly through each month at the monthly rates above. Each case stays open for a duration drawn from the three buckets. A mature office's open caseload is its monthly intake times the average months a case stays open.

Departing offices keep working their open cases until the transfer date, then hand the chosen share to Senator A. Transferred cases keep closing on their original schedule. From the intake stop until new offices start, the chosen share of the departing offices' would-be intake opens at Senator A instead. Senator A is assumed to close cases at its normal pace even when overloaded, so its peak is a floor.
